WHY WE EXIST

The Problem We Solve

Real challenges facing energy access in East Africa that drove us to build Ntronix.

Unreliable Grid Power

Frequent outages and load-shedding disrupt homes, schools, clinics, and businesses every day across Uganda and East Africa. Communities need dependable power they can control.

Expensive Imported Inverters

Solar inverters are imported at high cost, designed for foreign conditions, with no local repair or support available. This makes reliable solar power inaccessible to most households.

No Smart Energy Systems

There is virtually no indigenous power electronics manufacturing in East Africa. Available inverters offer no connectivity, no analytics, no remote control — users have zero visibility into their own energy data.

Purpose-Built Hardware

Each Volta powerwall uses dedicated processors for connectivity, solar charging, and AC generation — designed to work together seamlessly. The result is reliable, efficient power conversion with intelligent monitoring and protection built in at every level.

About Ntronix

Ntronix is a clean-energy technology startup focused on advanced powerwalls, hybrid inverters, and intelligent energy systems — designed and built in Uganda for African and global markets.

Ankunda Nelson — Founder & CEO

A Ugandan engineer, educator, and largely self-taught embedded systems specialist. Trained as a teacher of Physics and Mathematics, Nelson combines deep technical expertise with strong analytical skills developed through extensive self-driven learning in embedded systems, power electronics, and renewable energy. He has independently designed and developed working energy prototypes with a focus on efficient hardware-software integration.

Full-Stack Hardware Company

From circuit board design to firmware to mobile app development — we own the entire technology stack. Every component is engineered in-house with no outsourced modules.
