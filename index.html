WHY WE EXIST

Why Africa Needs Ntronix

600 million people in Sub-Saharan Africa lack reliable electricity. The solutions available are imported, overpriced, and lack connectivtiy. We're changing that.

The Grid Fails Daily

Load-shedding hits homes, hospitals, and businesses across East Africa every single day. People lose food, income, and productivity. Children study by candlelight. This is not acceptable in 2026.

Imports Drain the Economy

Every power station, powerwall, and energy system sold in East Africa is imported — at 2x-3x the cost, designed for foreign conditions, with zero local repair capability. When it breaks, you buy another import. Billions of shillings leave the continent every year.

Zero Local Innovation

There is virtually no indigenous smart energy manufacturing in all of East Africa. No Bluetooth. No app. No AI. No smart switches. No Volta AI engineer. Existing products are black boxes with no intelligence. Africa deserves better — and we are building it.

Engineering & Technology

Purpose-built hardware and software working together — intelligent power stations, smart switches, Volta AI, and a full energy ecosystem for homes and businesses.

ENGINEERED

Purpose-Built Hardware

Each Volta powerwall uses dedicated processors for connectivity, solar charging, and AC generation — designed to work together seamlessly. The result is reliable, efficient power conversion with intelligent monitoring and protection built in at every level.

Bluetooth + WiFi + Cloud

Encrypted Bluetooth pairing, secure cloud remote control, and wireless mesh networking for smart switches.

Smart Solar Charging

Advanced maximum power point tracking with >95% efficiency and support for LiFePO4, Li-Ion, Lead-Acid, AGM, and Gel batteries.

Safety & Protection

Multi-layer protection against overvoltage, overcurrent, overtemperature, and short circuits with automatic recovery.

Pure Sine Wave Output

Clean, stable AC power safe for sensitive electronics — laptops, medical devices, and appliances.

Secure Device Pairing

PIN-based pairing displayed on the device screen. Share access with family using invite codes with role-based permissions.

UPS Bypass Mode

Instant switchover keeps your devices running when the grid drops — available on the VC-3000.

OTA Firmware Updates

Remote firmware management with version history and model-specific selection via the mobile app.

About Ntronix

Ntronix is a clean-energy technology startup focused on intelligent powerwalls, portable power stations, smart switches, and the Volta AI engineer terminal — designed and built in Uganda for African and global markets.

Ankunda Nelson — Founder & CEO

A Ugandan engineer, educator, and largely self-taught embedded systems specialist. Trained as a teacher of Physics and Mathematics, Nelson combines deep technical expertise with strong analytical skills developed through extensive self-driven learning in embedded systems, power electronics, and renewable energy. He has independently designed and developed working energy prototypes with a focus on efficient hardware-software integration.
