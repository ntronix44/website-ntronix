Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information the Ntronix Volta website collects and how it's used. Ntronix ("we", "us") is based in Kampala, Uganda.

Information We Collect

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, general location, device type). This is aggregated, standard web analytics data.

Our contact form does not submit data to a Ntronix server. When you click "Send Message," it opens your own email application with the message pre-filled, and you choose to send it. We only receive what you actually send us by email.

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, or email directly, we receive whatever information you share with us in that conversation (name, phone number, email address, and the content of your message).

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries about products, partnerships, or investment
  • To understand overall site traffic and improve the website
  • To provide customer support for Ntronix products

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Analytics and is hosted on Netlify; both may process technical data (such as IP address) as part of providing their service. Their own privacy policies govern that processing.

Your Choices

You can decline Google Analytics tracking using your browser's Do Not Track or ad-blocking settings. You can choose what to share with us via email, phone, or WhatsApp.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to ntronixsmartcity@gmail.com.

This is a general-purpose policy and should be reviewed by a lawyer familiar with Ugandan data protection law before being treated as a complete legal document.
