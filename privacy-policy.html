Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Ntronix ("we", "us") is based in Kampala, Uganda. This policy covers the Ntronix Volta website and Volta AI Engineer applications, including the account service, command-line and desktop clients, and Visual Studio Code extension.

Information We Collect

Website and contact information

We use Google Analytics to understand website visits, such as pages viewed, general location, and device type. The website contact form opens your email application; it does not submit the message to a Ntronix server. If you contact us by email, phone, or WhatsApp, we receive the information and message you choose to share.

Account and sign-in information

When you create or use a Volta account, we process your email address, account identifier, and device or session identifiers needed to authenticate your installation. The browser-based account page uses session cookies to maintain a signed-in session. Email/password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; Ntronix does not store raw passwords in the Volta account tables. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides the basic profile and email information shown on its consent screen for account sign-in. We do not receive your Google password.

Usage and AI requests

To provide the service and enforce usage allowances, we process account or installation identifiers, token and AI-action usage totals, request-routing details such as provider or model, and plan or entitlement information.

When you ask Volta for help, your prompt and relevant editor context are sent to the Volta account API and the AI provider selected in the extension. If you have selected text, that selection may be included; otherwise, the extension may include up to the first 12,000 characters of the active editor document. The entire workspace is not automatically uploaded. Do not include passwords, API keys, or confidential code unless you are authorized to share it.

Optional cloud conversation history

Cloud history sync is optional. In the VS Code extension, if enabled, Volta stores visible conversation prompts and replies for syncing. This cloud history is not end-to-end encrypted. Active-editor context automatically added to model requests, project files, and tool results are not separately stored as cloud history. Turning sync off deletes the synced conversation copies.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure accounts and authenticate devices
  • To answer AI requests, measure usage, and administer account plans
  • To provide optional cloud conversation sync when you enable it
  • To respond to support requests and improve our products and website
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

Service Providers

Supabase provides authentication and account data services, and Render hosts the Volta account API. The AI provider selected in Volta (DeepSeek by default, or another configured provider such as Anthropic, Gemini, or OpenRouter) processes AI requests. Netlify hosts this website, and Google Analytics measures website traffic. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and Your Choices

We retain account, device, and usage information as needed to operate accounts, administer usage, and protect the service. Synced conversations remain until you delete them or turn off cloud history sync, which deletes the synced copies. You can use Volta without enabling cloud history. To ask about or request deletion of account information, contact us using the address below.

When using the extension, you choose what to ask Volta and can select the text to provide as context. If no text is selected, the extension may include the active file excerpt described above.

Security

Hosted account traffic uses HTTPS. The VS Code extension stores session credentials in VS Code SecretStorage and uses a short-lived authorization code with PKCE during sign-in. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email ntronixsmartcity@gmail.com.

This policy describes current product behavior and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed by a lawyer familiar with applicable Ugandan data-protection requirements.
